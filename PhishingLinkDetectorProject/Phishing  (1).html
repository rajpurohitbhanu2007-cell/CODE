About This Detector

This tool is designed to protect users from phishing attacks by performing a real-time analysis of URLs. It uses a scoring system based on several common indicators of malicious websites.

How It Works

The detector checks for:

  • Lack of HTTPS
  • Suspiciously long URLs
  • Presence in a known blacklist
  • Uncommon or misleading Top-Level Domains (TLDs)
  • Use of an IP address instead of a domain name
  • And several other risk factors...
