**Bottom line:** the fixes that *were* done are generally real and correct (not cosmetic) — the single-instance mutex, the FortiGate false-positive-login fix, the credential trust check, the idle-logout duplicate-thread fix, and the keepalive redirect-following fix are all solid. But well over half the list, including several of the other High-severity items (TLS verification still off everywhere, the proxy's SSRF/auth gaps, the proxy start()-failure handling), is untouched or only half-addressed. I wouldn't ship this as "all 60 bugs fixed."
